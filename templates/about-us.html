About Us

Welcome to our Student-Friendly Engineering Student Hub - your go-to resource for VTU Problem Solving.
We are a passionate team of engineers who have come together to make your academic journey smoother, more enjoyable, and, most importantly, successful.

Our Mission

Our mission is to simplify the lives of engineering students by providing them with easy-to-use, comprehensive, and accurate mathematical tools, along with other resources. We understand the challenges that students face in their studies, and our goal is to empower you with the knowledge and tools you need to excel in your engineering courses.

Existence

Engineering subjects can be daunting, and we've witnessed countless students struggle with the mathematical aspects of their studies. This is where we saw an opportunity to make a difference. We believe that everyone deserves access to high-quality resources, and our hub is designed to bridge the gap between complex subjects and student's needs.

Meet the Team

Our team comprises of 2 tech enthusiasts who share a common passion for education and technology. We've come together to create a tool that simplifies your learning journey.

Get in Touch

We are always eager to hear from you, whether it's for feedback, questions, or suggestions. Your input is invaluable in helping us make our calculator even better. Join us on our journey to make engineering math more accessible and less intimidating. Thank you for choosing our Student-Friendly Hub to support your academic success.
